**Rakhigarhi** (Heritage group) is the owner's Rakhigarhi Research Platform concept, absorbed into Maataa. It is a concept: it claims no foundation, institution, grant, booking, site access or verified collection, and every page says so.

## Tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | The four perspectives (native / local, ancestral interpretation, modern rural, research) and shortcuts. |
| **Sources** | The cited literature review with five references, and three source notes. |
| **News** | Demo press cards, labelled as placeholders. |
| **The site** | The seven mounds as illustrative markers, with a 200 m scale bar. Not survey data. |
| **India map** | Twelve sample heritage places as points. Search and filter by period. No boundaries are drawn. |
| **Research** | Proposed research and capability areas. |
| **Activities** | 25 proposed visit activities. **Add to draft** puts one in your itinerary draft. |
| **Itinerary** | Your drafts, the seven-day program, the two-day field visit and its budgets (below). |
| **Packages** | Workshop and documentary packages, including *The Line Producer: Documentary Series*. Add them to a draft too. |
| **Providers** | Your registry of people, vendors and services, and the auto itinerary. |
| **3D gallery** | Four synthetic shapes in a 3D viewer. Drag to turn them. |

## Itinerary

- **Draft plans**: rename a draft, set a date and group size, reorder or remove items and add notes. The total activity time is added up for you. Drafts are saved in Maataa.
- **Field visit (2–3 November 2026)**: the arrival flight, the cab fares from your screenshot, the route (opens OpenStreetMap), and the coverage still to quote.
- **Call sheet**: the Day 2 eight-hour production schedule, with the capture plan, crew handoff, kit list and go / no-go check.
- **Cost heads**: every line with its basis and status. Priced lines plus 10% contingency come to ₹35,137. Change the **scenario headcount** (1 to 20) to see group totals.
- **USD service menu**: tick crew services and set production days. These are kept separate from the INR budget and not converted.
- **Planning model**: the seven-day, six-night budget. Edit the adults, meals per day, vehicle per day and learning cost; the per-person totals update.

These are planning estimates from the rates you supplied, not quotes or bookings.

## Providers

Register a person, vendor or service with roles (for example *line producer, runner, vehicle, language expert, camera, drone*), a rate, the area served, certifications with expiry dates and a **verified** tick. The prototype needed an online sign-in for this; in Maataa the registry stays on this computer.

The **auto itinerary** proposes, for each role the field visit needs, the cheapest provider who is verified and whose certifications have not expired. Others are listed as needing review. It is a proposal only: nothing is booked or sent.

> **Note** The prototype's activity banners were AI-generated concept art; Maataa shows plain colour panels instead. Make your own artwork in Image Studio if you need it, and label it as illustration.
