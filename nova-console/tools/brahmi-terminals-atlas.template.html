Brahmi Terminal Atlas

Every vowel and consonant, thinned to its centre line. Stroke ends are found automatically; the ones in the lower 40% of the letter are its bottom terminals. The magnet rule then gives a snap point, which is tested against where the font's designer attached the vowel signs u and ū.

Measured on one modern typeface, not on inscriptions. The odd/even rule uses the corrected formulas: the midpoint of two terminals is (xA + xB) / 2, and with y growing downward the bottom of a circle is at θ = π/2.
